What does a pathology lab do?

A pathology lab tests samples of blood, urine, stool and tissue to help your doctor find the cause of a problem, check your health and monitor a condition. Our trained team collects the sample safely, the lab tests it with careful quality checks, and you get a clear report. Run by Ibad Khan (B.M.L.T. Path) in Sarai Tareen, Sambhal.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to fast before a test?

Some tests (like fasting sugar) need an overnight fast, others do not. Ask us when you book.

Is home sample collection available?

Yes. Call or WhatsApp us to book.

How do I get my report?

E-reports are sent to your phone or email.

Is the report valid for medico-legal purposes?

No, reports are not valid for medico-legal purposes.
